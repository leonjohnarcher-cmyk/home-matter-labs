Pulmonology Focus

A Good Doctor Sees Home First, Before You

Respiratory baseline health starts directly where your patients live. 80% of pulmonary triggers are completely environmental.

In Respiratory Care, the Environment is the Trigger: Asthma Flare-ups • COPD Progression • Chronic Allergies • Recurrent Airway Infections • Sleep Apnea Disruption

Identify Root Triggers

Isolate particle spikes (PM2.5/PM10), chemical off-gassing (VOCs/Formaldehyde), and hidden fungal spores.

Prevent Exacerbations

Objective indoor environment assessment charts minimize sudden ER re-admissions and severe asthma relapses.

Calibrated Interventions

Provide targeted data readouts to back your clean-air advice, clinical therapies, and indoor humidity remediation plans.
