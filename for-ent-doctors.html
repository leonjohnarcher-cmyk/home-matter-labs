ENT Focus Area

A Healthy Environment Protects What You Hear, Smell & Breathe

80% of perennial ear, nose, and throat problems are heavily influenced or exacerbated by home environmental exposure factors.

Common ENT Home Triggers

  • 🍄 Mold & Fungal Spores: Triggers persistent sinusitis, nasal blockages, and allergies.
  • 🕷️ Dust Mites & Allergens: Causes sneezing, itchy nasal pathways, and Eustachian tube dysfunction.
  • 💨 Air Pollutants: PM2.5, VOCs, and chemical gases cause vocal strain and throat irritation.
  • 🔊 Poor Acoustics & Noise: Elevated noise pollution degrades healing sleep cycles.
