One-Page Clinical Overview

Evidence-based home environment insights for better patient outcomes.

Home Matter Lab Test is a comprehensive environmental home health assessment that evaluates key indoor factors. The resulting data helps physicians complement clinical evaluations beyond the clinic walls.

Targeted Portals: 👂 Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT) Portal 🫁 Pulmonology & Lung Portal 👶 Pediatrics & Child Health Portal

Doctor Engagement Flow

Doctors treat the patient and Home Matter Labs assesses the home environment.

01

Doctor Consultation

Evaluates patient via symptoms, history, and examination. If relevant, prescribes the test.

02

Patient Registration

Patient contacts the lab OR doctor routes prescription directly to trigger operational onboarding flow.

03

Home Assessment

Lab schedules home inspection tracking using advanced calibration devices and captures raw samples.

04

Laboratory Analysis

Collected materials run through verified NABL diagnostic channels to secure data parameters safely.

05

Report Sharing

A detailed and structured environmental health portfolio compiles securely for family and referring doctor review.

06

Remediation Consulting

Lab field consultants offer clear, actionable mitigation steps to control allergens and toxic load zones.

07

Doctor Review

Physician analyzes the environmental asset map patterns next to external clinical signs and symptoms.

08

Clinical Follow-Up

Clinical advice, treatment calibrations, and patient lifestyle changes align for better baseline survival health.
